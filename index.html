Why existing apps don't solve this

Every bill-management app — Rocket Money, Mint, YNAB — requires connecting your bank account. They're built for one person managing their own money. None of them let a caregiver monitor an elder's bills without the elder giving up full account access.

IntakeData works differently. The elder controls what gets forwarded. The caregiver sees what needs attention. No bank login. No account takeover. Just a simple email forward.

And unlike bank-connected apps, we catch what arrives by paper mail — the bills that never touch a digital feed.
